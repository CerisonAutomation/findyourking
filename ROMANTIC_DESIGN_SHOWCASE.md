## ✨ ANIMATION & EFFECTS

### Entrance Animations
- **Duration:** 0.8s with cubic-bezier easing
- **Opacity:** 0 → 1
- **Transform:** Y-axis 30px → 0px
- **Delay progression:** 0.2s → 0.7s for elements

### Hover Effects
- **Scale:** 1.0 → 1.02 or 1.05
- **Shadow:** Elevation increase
- **Y-axis:** Slight upward movement
- **Transition:** 0.3s ease

### Floating Animations
- **Duration:** 6-8 seconds
- **Motion:** Y-axis ±20px
- **Rotation:** ±5 degrees
- **Easing:** easeInOut (smooth natural motion)

### Glass Morphism
- **Backdrop Filter:** blur(20px)
- **Background:** Semi-transparent white/dark
- **Border:** Subtle transparent white border
- **Box Shadow:** Depth + inset highlight

### Loading States
- **Spinner:** Rotating border animation
- **Pulse:** Green indicator dot animation
- **Duration:** 0.8s for smooth appearance
